About

I'm a broadcast presenter and writer drawn to emotion and the human side of every story. At Sweet FM, I bring warmth and depth to the stories I share on air, from everyday life to the bigger conversations shaping our world. I believe every story deserves to be told with honesty and heart.

Broadcasting began with a passion to be heard, to speak and to make an impact. Training at the National Broadcast Academy in Lagos and interning alongside veterans in the field sharpened my skills and left me curious to learn and do more.

Why I Tell Stories

Stories are lenses to the soul. They show us what people carry, what they feel, and what they hope for — and every time one is told well, someone feels seen.
