About

Fatimah is a passionate broadcast presenter and writer who writes about emotions and human feeling. As a presenter at Sweet FM, she brings warmth and depth to the stories she shares on air, drawn to the human side of every issue — from everyday life to the bigger conversations shaping our world. She believes every story deserves to be told with honesty and heart.

Broadcasting was born out of a passion to be heard, to speak and to make an impact. Training at the National Broadcast Academy in Lagos and interning with veterans in the field sharpened her skills and left her curious to learn and do more.

Why I Tell Stories

Stories are lenses to the soul. They show us what people carry, what they feel, and what they hope for — and every time one is told well, someone feels seen.
